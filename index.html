Our specialties

Muscle-based care for speaking and eating

Everything we do comes back to the same set of muscles — the ones a child uses to form sounds, to chew and swallow safely, and to rest the tongue and lips at the right place. Here is where each of those lives.

  • Pediatric Speech Therapy

    Individualized therapy for speech delay, articulation, childhood apraxia and dyspraxia, phonological and motor speech disorders, and overall speech intelligibility.

    What we treat in speech

  • Feeding Therapy

    Support for picky eating, food refusals and aversions, texture transitions, gagging, chewing and drinking skills, and the move from tube feeding to eating by mouth.

    What we treat in feeding

  • Oral Motor & Myofunctional Therapy

    Muscle-based therapy for tongue thrust, open mouth breathing, drooling and tongue positioning — coordinated with your child’s dentist or orthodontist.

    What we treat in oral motor

Our office

A small practice with one deep specialty

Alphabet Soup is a small Norwalk practice built around a single area of expertise: the muscles and sensory systems behind speaking and eating. Bonnie Wilder and Malka Strasberg have spent decades in oral motor, feeding, and myofunctional therapy, working with children from birth through age 21 — often alongside a family’s pediatrician, dentist, or orthodontist. Sessions are one-to-one and unhurried, families are part of the work, and progress is measured in the everyday things: a clearer word, a new food, a calmer mealtime.
