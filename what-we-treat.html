What we treat

What we treat

Speech, feeding, and oral motor or myofunctional therapy for infants, children, teens, and young adults.

Speech Therapy Feeding Therapy Oral Motor / Myofunctional Therapy

Speech

Speech Therapy

We evaluate and treat:

  • Delayed speech
  • Articulation
  • Apraxia / dyspraxia
  • Phonological disorders
  • Motor speech disorders
  • Speech intelligibility

PROMPT therapy

PROMPT — Prompts for Restructuring Oral Muscular Phonetic Targets — is a tactile-kinesthetic approach that uses touch cues on the face, jaw, and neck to guide the movements that make speech. It is one of the named approaches we use.

Feeding

Feeding Therapy

We evaluate and treat:

  • Food refusals: refusing all food or most foods.
  • Texture transitions: difficulty accepting foods with a certain texture.
  • Limited food repertoire: having a very limited list of ‘acceptable’ foods they will eat.
  • Gagging: when the gag reflex prevents enjoying food or trying new foods.
  • Food stuffing: putting an inappropriate amount of food in their mouth.
  • Behavioral feeders: behaviors around eating that limit the way they eat.
  • Failure to Thrive: a diagnosis where your child isn’t gaining enough weight and falling off the growth charts.
  • Tube feeders: supporting the transition from feeding from a tube to eating orally.
  • Sensory and muscle based feeding difficulties
  • Picky eating
  • Food aversions
  • Transitioning to textures
  • Cup and straw drinking
  • Chewing skills

SOS therapy

The SOS Approach to Feeding — Sequential Oral Sensory — is a structured, play-based program that lets a child get comfortable with a new food one small step at a time, from tolerating it on the table through touching, smelling, tasting, and eating it. It is one of the named approaches we use.

Oral motor

Oral Motor Therapy / Myofunctional Therapy

We evaluate and treat:

  • Tongue thrust
  • Open mouth breathing
  • Drooling
  • Tongue positioning
  • Support before, during and after palatal expansion and/or braces

Working with your dental team

We work closely with dentists and orthodontists, and make referrals to airway-centric dentists and orthodontists as needed.
